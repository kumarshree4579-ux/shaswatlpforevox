Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our cloud PBX and VoIP solutions.

Why Hotels and hospitality Are Choosing VoIP for Smarter Communication?

Hotels and hospitality businesses choose VoIP because it reduces communication costs and enhances the guest experience through reliable features like wake-up calls, custom IVR menus, and direct-to-staff messaging.

What is VoIP, and why choose it over traditional phone systems?

VoIP (Voice over Internet Protocol) allows voice calls to be transmitted over the internet instead of traditional phone lines. It offers cost efficiency, flexibility, and scalability, making it a modern replacement for analog systems.

How does a VoIP phone system work in a facility?

A VoIP system converts voice into digital signals, transmitting calls via the internet. It uses PBX functionality, IVR menus, and call routing to manage guest and staff communications efficiently.

What features should a hospitality/healthcare/senior living VoIP system include?

Key features include IVR menus, call routing, voicemail, multilingual support, auto-attendants, wake-up calls, analytics, and call forwarding.

Is VoIP reliable for both small and large facilities?

Yes. VoIP is highly scalable and adaptable, making it reliable for small boutique properties as well as large multi-property hotel chains, hospitals, and senior living facilities.

Does VoIP support emergency dialing (E911)?

Yes, most VoIP providers support Enhanced 911 (E911), allowing emergency services to locate callers quickly, which is crucial for healthcare and hospitality environments.

What happens if the internet connection fails? Is VoIP still usable?

In the event of an internet outage, the VoIP system will automatically switch to the 4G backup router/data connection or an available backup internet line, ensuring continuous communication.

Can existing analog phone lines be used with VoIP systems?

Yes. With the use of analog telephone adapters (ATAs) or compatible gateways, facilities can connect legacy analog phones to a VoIP system, reducing the need for a full equipment overhaul.

Is VoIP secure? What are the risks?

VoIP can be highly secure when using encryption and firewalls. Risks like eavesdropping, spoofing, and packet loss exist but are mitigated with proper security protocols and monitoring.

What kind of phones are needed for a VoIP system? Can we use softphones or mobile apps?

Yes! VoIP systems are highly versatile. You can use traditional IP desk phones, wireless cordless phones, softphones installed on your computers (Windows/Mac), and mobile apps on smartphones (iOS/Android), allowing your team to communicate seamlessly from anywhere.
